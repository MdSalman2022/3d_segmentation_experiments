
================================================================================
                    KiTS23 DATASET ANALYSIS SUMMARY
================================================================================

DATASET INFORMATION:
- Name: Kidney Tumor Segmentation Challenge 2023 (KiTS23)
- Total Cases: 489 CT scans
- Task: Multi-class semantic segmentation
- Classes: Background (0), Kidney (1), Tumor (2), Cyst (3)

KEY CHALLENGES:
1. Class Imbalance: Tumors and cysts are much smaller than kidneys
2. Variable Sizes: Wide range of structure volumes
3. Complex Anatomy: Tumors embedded within kidney tissue

PREPROCESSING PIPELINE:
- Resampling to isotropic spacing (1.0-1.5mm)
- CT windowing: [-200, 300] HU
- Normalization: Zero-mean, unit-variance
- Data augmentation: Rotation, scaling, elastic deformation

MODEL APPROACH:
- Architecture: nnU-Net (3D Full Resolution)
- Training: 1000 epochs with polynomial LR decay
- Loss: Dice + Cross-Entropy (handles class imbalance)
- Ensemble: Multiple configurations for robustness

TARGET METRICS:
- Kidney Dice: ≥ 0.90
- Tumor Dice: ≥ 0.70
- Cyst Dice: ≥ 0.50

STATUS: Preprocessing in progress, training to follow.
